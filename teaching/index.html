CSE452: Optical Fiber Communication @ DIU

Fall 2026

This course will aim to introduce students to the fundamentals of fiber optic communications, which constitute the backbone of the internet. The course will start with a refresher on the operation of key components needed for an effective fiber optic communication system and then show how these components interact at a system level. Finally, the course will conclude with an outlook for future research in extending the capabilities of these networks to higher bandwidths and quantum-secured communications.

CSE474: VLSI Design @ DIU

Fall 2026

This course introduces the fundamental principles and practices of VLSI technology and design. It covers IC fabrication steps, MOS transistor theory, threshold voltage, body effect, I-V and C-V characteristics, scaling, and CMOS inverter static and dynamic behavior. Students learn delay modeling, logical effort, layout and stick diagrams, logic design optimization, combinational and dynamic logic families, power and energy trade-offs, sequential logic, memory, HDL/Verilog, finite state machines, and interconnect effects. The course emphasizes analysis, design, and optimization of digital CMOS circuits and subsystems.

CSE215: Electronic Devices and Circuits @ DIU

Fall 2026

This subject is classified under the Core Technology group and intended to teach the students about theory, concepts and principles of operation of various electronics devices (Hardware) related to their use and working in electronic systems and applications. The Knowledge and skills of Electronics Devices and Circuits will help the students in Digital Electronics, Data Communications, Embedded Systems, IoT and Robotics.

CSE325: Instrumentation and Control @ DIU

Summer 2026

This 14-week course provides a foundational understanding of both instrumentation and control systems. Students will explore the fundamental components and methods of electrical and electronic measurement, learning to analyze sensor data and system models. The curriculum bridges theory with practical application, covering topics like data acquisition, analog-to-digital/digital-to-analog conversion, and basic control logic. By the end of the course, students are expected to not only understand but also design simple system architectures and implement basic monitoring and control applications using appropriate tools and computing platforms.

CSE121: Electrical Circuits with Lab @ DIU

Summer 2025, Summer 2026

This course introduces students to the fundamental principles of electrical circuits and their applications. The course will cover the basics of circuit analysis techniques, including Kirchhoff's laws, Ohm's law, nodal and mesh analysis, and Thevenin’s and Norton’s equivalent circuits. It will also introduce students to AC circuit analysis, including phasor, impedance, and RLC circuits.
